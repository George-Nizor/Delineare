## 5. Why the current structure fails

**Scope.** It is an encyclopedia, not a course.
- 96 modules, including Bargue sight-size (20 to 80 h), insects, reptiles and fish, ornament, curvilinear perspective, orthographic projection, Copic markers, Krita file formats, style development and portfolio.
- The spine alone is 251 to 517 h, plus 75 to 190 h of ongoing practice. All modules together come to 668 to 1,546 h.
- At 3 hours a week that is 3 to 10 years.

**Granularity is wildly uneven.**
- Modules range from `baseline-imagination-check` (1 to 2 h) to `atelier-bargue-cast-sight-size` (20 to 80 h) and `des-visual-development-workflow` (20 to 50 h).
- Phases 1 and 2 hold five spine modules on imagination, ideation and memory before the learner has drawn a box: baseline, ideation-and-prompts, surreal-ideation, reference-visual-library and visual-memory. Four of them are flagged ongoing.

**Overlap survives the merge.**
- Values: `light-values-squint`, `light-value-composition` and `composition-and-thumbnails`.
- Expression: `fig-features-expression-hair` and `des-expression-acting`.
- Line weight: `found-line-weight` and `inking-line-art`.
- Gesture: four modules (`fig-gesture-line-of-action`, `fig-timed-gesture-progression`, `fig-life-drawing-session-routine`, `fig-dynamic-poses-interaction`).

**Phase order fights the prerequisite graph.**
- `light-form-on-basic-forms` needs only 23 to 46 h of prerequisites, but in phase order it comes after 194 to 403 spine hours. Shading arrives after figure, head, hands, drapery and anatomy.
- `fig-head-construction` needs 30 to 64 h of prerequisites, but in phase order it comes after 164 to 339 h.
- Colour theory is "essential" yet is the very last spine module.
- The longest prerequisite chain is 9 deep. `des-character-design-process` depends on 20 modules (102 to 213 h).

**Five overlapping ways to classify one learner's path.** Phase, strand, tier (essential / deepening / stretch / optional), track (core / play / optional), and the spine and ongoing flags. The "play" track holds only 3 modules.

**Time estimates are invented.**
- `CURRICULUM.md` itself says research agents designed the hours, not a school.
- Ranges are typically 2 times wide and up to 5 times (`media-sampler`, 6 to 30 h).
- `ongoing_hours` is null for four ongoing modules.
- Doses and minutes disagree: in `light-form-on-basic-forms`, "Stack and overlap" has a dose of "3 drawings, 90 min" but `minutes: 30`.

**Each module page is overloaded.**
- 5 to 11 practice items, 3 to 7 done-when checks and 2 to 12 resources per module, each resource with a role and a note.
- 594 practice items add up to 313 hours for a single pass.
- 435 done-when checks, 308 of them self-judged.
- About 60,000 words of module text.

**The research hedges were shipped to the learner.**
- 119 of 351 `free_notes` and 63 of 465 attachment notes carry research-process caveats. Examples: "search-result evidence; not opened", "exercise names and counts were not verified here, so check them against the page".
- 12 modules have thin free coverage and needed original lessons, which is a sign the scope runs past the free material.
- Supplementary attachments (198) outnumber core ones (105).

## 6. Practice material that can be reused

**Practice items (594) are the best asset in the repo.**
- The schema is clean and directly reusable. Kinds: 258 exercise, 191 study, 96 drill, 43 play, 6 benchmark. 91 use ladder timers. Reference kinds: 203 none, 128 life, 68 photo, 63 prompt, 48 museum, 33 tool, 29 pose-site, 22 memory.
- The content is concrete and drawable. Strong examples:
  - `obs-measuring`: chair gap drawing, angle drill, unit-and-ratios table, reverse check.
  - `found-line-control`: the point-to-point line page.
  - `fig-timed-gesture-progression`: the 29-minute ladder (10 poses at 30 s, 6 at 1 min, 4 at 2 min, 2 at 5 min) and "one-lens sessions".
  - `light-form-on-basic-forms`: "predict, then check" for cast shadows and the four-value egg.
  - `invent-objects-1`: "study, hide, redraw".
- Mine 100 to 150 of these for the new course.

**Revisits (one per module).** 3 to 10 minute cold redraws, ready-made for a "practise whenever you like" pool.

**Measured done-when checks (70).** These make good self-checks:
- 7 of 10 lines land on both dots.
- Ratios within about a tenth of the real ones.
- Extended box edges meet within 1 cm on 7 of 10 boxes.
- The reflected light checks correctly in a greyscale photo.

**Other reusable pieces:**
- The baseline imagination set: ten prompts (a person running, a dog, a chair, a car, a tree, a room, a street corner, a hand holding a cup, a face looking up, an invented creature). Reuse it as the before-and-after test.
- The 9 benchmarks in `curriculum/v2/benchmarks.json`, which could become 4 or 5 checkpoint lessons.
- The 10-minute `short_session`.

**Code worth keeping:**
- `src/tools/LightTool.tsx` (three.js): lamp or sun light; direction, height and room-light sliders; three form sets; posterised value view. Works and is reusable.
- `src/tools/FormTool.tsx`: box, cylinder or slab in 1-, 2- or 3-point presets; a seeded random-view drill; horizon and vanishing-point overlay. Works and is reusable.
- Not built yet: the mannequin and grid tools, which 10 practice items reference. Report 04 also specs hand and head tools.
- `src/references/prompts.ts`: an invention prompt generator (53 things, 22 qualities, 9 views).
- `src/references/museums.ts`: the AIC and Cleveland searches work, with a credit line and 30-day cache. The Met search needs moving to v1.1.
- Two built-in CC0 SVG renders (egg and box).
- The `library` block (54 entries) has usable licence notes for pose sites, photo banks, museums and Incompetech graph paper (CC0).
