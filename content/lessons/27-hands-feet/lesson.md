# Hands

A hand is easier to draw from imagination when you build it from simple forms. In this lesson you'll draw a hand as a box palm with a wrist, jointed fingers and a thumb, using Proko's process.

![A hand from the back and a hand with curled fingers, each built from a box palm, cylinder fingers, a thumb and a wrist box.](example)

## Steps

1. **Decide.** Choose the viewing angle, whether you see the palm or the back, and what each finger and the thumb is doing. Picture it before you mark the page. Proko suggests using your own hand to work it out.

   ![Three small hands: the back of the hand, the palm and curled fingers.](decide)

2. **Palm.** Draw a box about as wide as it is tall. Curve its front plane, since the bones on the little-finger side are shorter. Divide the front plane into 4 parts, one per finger.

   ![A palm box with a curved front plane split into four parts.](palm)

3. **Fingers.** Draw a mitten shape or a light line for each finger. Do the pinky and index finger first, and fit the middle two between. Build each of the 12 segments as a cylinder, as Proko does.

   ![A hand with three cylinders for each finger, twelve segments in all.](fingers)

4. **Thumb.** Draw a stretched triangular box at its base, a cylinder for the first segment and a wedge for the tip. Bridgman also sees the base of the thumb as a pyramid.

   ![A hand with the thumb built from a triangular box, a cylinder and a wedge.](thumb)

5. **Wrist.** Draw the wrist as a narrower box. Place the hand slightly lower than the forearm so there is a small step down, as Proko shows, following Bridgman.

   ![A forearm box, a narrower wrist box and the palm, showing the step down to the hand.](wrist)

Proko teaches this process in his lesson on drawing hands from imagination.

## Common mistake

![Left, four fingers as tubes of one length. Right, fingers that taper, with the middle finger longest.](mistake)

Drawing the four fingers as tubes of one length. Vanderpoel notes that the fingers taper together and the tip of the middle finger, the longest, is the apex. Draw the curved knuckle line first, then give each finger its own length.

## Checking your page

Check each hand against the steps:

1. The palm is a box, with a curved front plane split into four parts.
2. Each finger has three segments; the thumb has a box, cylinder and wedge.
3. The middle finger reaches farthest and the pinky side is shorter.
4. The wrist box is narrower than the palm.

Draw your own hand in the same pose and compare.
