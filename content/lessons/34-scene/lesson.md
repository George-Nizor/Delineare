# A scene from imagination

This lesson puts the course together in one invented picture: a street or room with people and shadows. You'll plan it with thumbnails, set up the perspective, place figures at the right scale and add shadows. The steps use methods from lessons 19, 31 and 32.

## Steps

1. **Plan.** Draw six three-value thumbnails of a street or room, as in lesson 32. Choose the clearest and copy it large, about half a page wide.

2. **Set the space.** Draw the horizon and two vanishing points, then build the buildings or walls as boxes, drawn through. Put the horizon where your thumbnail has it.

3. **Scale the people.** Draw Storey's height scale from lesson 31. Place at least four figures at different distances. Build each as a mannequin, then add shape.

4. **Cast the shadows.** Choose a sun at 45 degrees to one side. Build shadows for the boxes and for each figure, using the tips method from lesson 19.

5. **Set the values.** Fill the shadows with your dark tone, keeping the three values from your thumbnail. Leave the lights as paper.

## Common mistake

Drawing everything at the same level of detail. When each building and figure gets the same attention, the thumbnail's plan disappears. Keep the main subject the most detailed and leave the rest flat.

## Checking your page

Hold the page at arm's length and check these:

1. The scene still reads like your chosen thumbnail.
2. Every building edge runs to one of the two vanishing points.
3. Nearer figures are taller than farther ones, with heads near the horizon.
4. All shadows fall the same way.
