# A scene from imagination

This lesson puts the course together in one invented picture: a street with people and shadows. You'll plan it with thumbnails, set up the perspective, place figures at the right scale and add shadows.

![A street with two box buildings, four people at different heights and dark shadows falling to the right.](example)

## Steps

1. **Plan.** Draw six three-value thumbnails of a street, as in lesson 32. Choose the clearest and copy it about half a page wide.

   ![Six thumbnails of a street in three values, with one ringed.](plan)

2. **Set the space.** Draw the horizon where your thumbnail has it, then two vanishing points. Build the buildings as boxes whose edges run to them, and draw through each box.

   ![A horizon and two box buildings, with dashed lines from their corners running to the vanishing points.](space)

3. **Scale the people.** Draw Storey's height scale from lesson 31. Place at least four figures at different distances, each at the height the scale gives. Build each as a mannequin, then add shape.

   ![The street with a height scale at the left edge and four people whose heights come from it.](people)

4. **Cast the shadows.** Put the sun at 45 degrees to one side. From the top of each vertical edge draw a ray down at 45 degrees, and from its foot a floor line sideways. They meet at the shadow's tip.

   ![The street with dashed 45 degree rays from the top of each building and person meeting the floor, and the shadows between.](shadows)

5. **Set the values.** Use the three values from your thumbnail: dark for the shadows, a middle tone for the faces turned from the sun, paper for the lights.

   ![The finished street in three values, with dark shadows and middle-tone walls.](values)

Storey says that with the sun at 45 degrees in the plane of the picture, each shadow is as long as the object is tall. The thumbnail method is Proko's.

## Common mistake

![Left, every building and figure drawn with the same detail. Right, detail only on the main building.](mistake)

Drawing everything at the same level of detail. When each building and figure gets the same attention, the thumbnail's plan disappears. Keep the main subject the most detailed and leave the rest flat.

## Checking your page

Look at the whole page and check these:

1. The scene still reads like your chosen thumbnail.
2. Every building edge runs to one of the two vanishing points.
3. Nearer figures are taller than farther ones.
4. All shadows fall the same way.
5. Every shadow is as long as the thing that casts it is tall.
