# Cylinders

A cylinder is two ellipses joined by straight sides, and the ellipses must line up. In this lesson you'll draw a cylinder at any angle by building it around a straight line through its centre. Then you'll check how well the ellipses line up.

![A page of five cylinders at different angles, each built round a dashed axis.](example)

## Steps

1. **Draw the axis.** Ghost and draw a straight line longer than the cylinder will be. Drawabox calls it the minor axis. It cuts every ellipse along the cylinder into two equal halves.

   ![A single straight line running up and to the right, longer than the cylinder to come.](axis)

2. **Place the first ellipse.** Draw an ellipse near one end of the line, with the whole line passing through it. Don't put it right on the end point.

   ![An ellipse near the left end of the axis, the line running through it and past its edge.](first)

3. **Place the second.** Draw an ellipse at the other end. Make a farther ellipse a little rounder than the first, and a nearer one a little narrower. Drawabox calls how round an ellipse is its degree.

   ![Two ellipses on the axis. The nearer one is narrower and the farther one a little rounder.](second)

4. **Join the sides.** Ghost and draw a straight line between the matching edges of the two ellipses, one on each side. These lines share a vanishing point with the axis.

   ![A cylinder whose two sides and axis, extended in dashes, head for one vanishing point off the page.](sides)

5. **Check.** Take a ruler and a pen of another colour. Find the line that cuts each ellipse into two equal halves across its narrow direction as exactly as you can, then compare it with your axis.

   ![A cylinder with its dashed axis and, in a second colour, the true halving line of each ellipse, a little off the axis.](check)

Drawabox gives this method in its 250 Cylinder Challenge.

## Common mistake

![Left, a cylinder with parallel sides and two ellipses of the same width. Right, a cylinder whose sides converge and whose far ellipse is rounder.](mistake)

The most common mistake is drawing the sides parallel on the page. That sends the vanishing point to infinity. Drawabox says unless the cylinder points exactly across your view, the sides must converge, even if only a little.

## Checking your page

Look at each cylinder and check these:

1. Both ellipses are smooth and drawn through.
2. The axis cuts each ellipse into two equal halves, or close to it.
3. The farther ellipse is rounder than the nearer one.
4. The sides converge, even if only slightly.

Being a little off is normal. The check shows you which way.
