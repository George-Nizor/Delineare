# Cast shadows

A shadow follows rules, so you can work out its shape instead of guessing. In this lesson you'll build the shadow of a box from the sun and from a lamp, using the method G. A. Storey sets out in his book on perspective.

![Two drawings of the same box, one with the parallel-edged shadow of the sun and one with the widening shadow of a lamp.](example)

## Steps

1. **Draw the box.** Draw a box standing on the floor with its front face square to you. Draw the horizon, and mark the vanishing point its side edges run to.

   ![A box on the floor, the horizon, and the vanishing point its side edges run to.](box)

2. **Place the sun.** Storey treats the sun's rays as parallel. With the sun level with the picture and 45 degrees up, a shadow on the floor is as long as the object is tall.

   ![A sun at the upper left with three parallel rays at 45 degrees, and a box.](sun)

3. **Find the tips.** From the top of each right-hand vertical edge, draw a ray down at 45 degrees. From the base of the same edge, draw a floor line sideways. They meet at that edge's shadow tip.

   ![Two rays from the top corners and two floor lines from the base corners, meeting at two shadow tips.](tips)

4. **Join and fill.** Join the tips with lines that run to the box's own vanishing point, as Storey does. Fill the shape with one flat dark tone.

   ![The shadow filled in, its receding edge heading for the vanishing point.](fill)

5. **Use a lamp.** The foot of a lamp is the spot on the floor under it. Draw floor lines from the foot through each base corner, and rays from the lamp over each top corner. They cross at the tips.

   ![A lamp with its foot on the floor, floor lines through the base corners, rays over the top corners, and a shadow that widens away from the box.](lamp)

## Common mistake

![Left, a lamp's shadow with parallel edges. Right, a lamp's shadow whose edges spread away from the box.](mistake)

The most common mistake is drawing a lamp's shadow with parallel edges. Storey says a lamp's rays diverge, so its shadow spreads. Only the sun's rays are parallel, and only then are the shadow's outlines parallel in space.

## Checking your page

Check each box against these:

1. The sun shadow is as long as the box is tall.
2. Every floor line starts at the base of the edge it belongs to.
3. The lamp shadow widens away from the box.
4. The shadow's receding edges run to the box's own vanishing point.
