# Still life

A still life brings the course together: you measure, block in, find shadow shapes and set values on one subject. In this lesson you'll draw three objects from life in about an hour, working from the large shape down to the tone.

![Three objects on a table, a box, a cup and a ball, lit from the left, drawn in paper, one middle tone and one dark.](example)

## Steps

1. **Set up.** Put two or three objects close together on a table. Light them strongly from one side, never from the front, as Speed advises. Ruskin keeps direct sun off and puts the light on your left.

   ![Seen from above, a lamp to the left of a table with three objects and you sitting in front.](setup)

2. **Block in.** Draw the envelope, the straight-sided shape around the whole group, as Ctrl+Paint teaches. Then fix the main points against one vertical line, as Speed does, and block each object in with straight lines.

   ![The envelope round three objects, a block for each, and a vertical line to measure from.](block)

3. **Check.** Step back and flip the drawing in a mirror, or lean back and squint, as Proko suggests. Compare it with the group and fix the largest error first.

   ![A drawing and its mirror image, with a line level with the top of the box showing the cup is too short.](check)

4. **Map the shadows.** Squint. Draw the edges of each shadow and cast shadow in straight lines, as Speed does, and fill them with one flat dark tone before you add any halftone.

   ![Three objects with every shadow and cast shadow filled in one flat dark.](shadows)

5. **Set the values.** Speed reduces a group to three tones: lights, half tones and shadows. Leave the lights as paper and add one middle tone between them and the dark. In pen, use hatching. Give the table its tone too.

   ![The same objects in three values: paper, a middle tone and the dark.](values)

## Common mistake

![Left, one box finished and a ball with no room left. Right, all three objects blocked in together.](mistake)

Finishing one object before you place the others. Speed warns that if the foundations are in the wrong place, the whole structure has to be torn down. Block in the whole group, and check it, before you shade anything.

## Checking your page

When you finish, step back and check these:

1. Each object's height, measured with a pencil, matches the group.
2. Every shadow and cast shadow falls away from the light.
3. The shadow shapes hold one flat dark, with no halftone inside.
4. Squinting at the page shows three values.
