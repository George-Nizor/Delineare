# Contour lines on round forms

Lines that run across a surface show which way it curves. In this lesson you'll draw sausage shapes and wrap contour lines around them so they look solid. Drawabox uses "contour line" for a line across a surface. It isn't the edge-following contour from lesson 3.

![A page of four sausages, three with a spine and contour ellipses and one with contour curves.](example)

## Steps

1. **Draw a sausage.** Draw two equal round ends joined by a middle of constant width, bent any way you like. Avoid ends of different sizes, flattened ends, or a middle that swells or narrows.

   ![A bent sausage with two equal dashed circles at its ends and a bracket showing its constant width.](sausage)

2. **Add a spine.** Draw a line through the centre of the sausage. It marks the **minor axis** of each ellipse you add: the line that cuts an ellipse in half across its narrow side.

   ![The sausage with a line down its centre.](spine)

3. **Draw contour ellipses.** Add a few ellipses around the sausage, each cut in half by the spine. Draw each through twice and keep it snug between the edges. Change each width a little: the further away ones are slightly wider.

   ![A sausage with five ellipses wrapped around it, each cut in half by the spine and widening toward the far end.](ellipses)

4. **Try contour curves.** On a second page, draw only the visible part of each contour line. Overshoot a little, so the curve hooks round the edge onto the far side.

   ![A sausage with five curves, each hooking round the edge.](curves)

5. **Cap a tip.** If one end points toward you, draw a small ellipse on it. You can see the whole of that end, so the whole ellipse is drawn.

   ![A sausage with its near end facing the viewer, capped with a small whole ellipse.](cap)

Drawabox, whose method this is, assigns one page of ellipses and one of curves.

## Common mistake

![Left, small upright ellipses floating inside a sausage. Right, ellipses that touch both edges and change width along the form.](mistake)

The most common mistake is contour lines that float. They hang inside the form without touching its edges, all at the same width. Drawabox asks for lines pinched snugly between the edges, with the width shifting along the form.

## Checking your page

Look at one page at a time and check these:

1. Each contour line touches both edges of the sausage, or stays close inside.
2. The spine cuts every ellipse into two equal halves.
3. The ellipses are not all the same width.
4. On the curves page, each curve hooks round the edge.

If your curves look flat, overshoot them more.
